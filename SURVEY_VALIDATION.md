## Arrow-reading model (what the player sees)

The player does not compare whole trails: they read the small push arrows drawn on each trail, and extend them by eye. This model reads those arrows with a **careful** error (8 degrees of bias per trail, 6 of jitter per arrow) or **by eye** (12 and 10), refuses lines that run within about 11 degrees of parallel, and counts a read as right only if the *kind* is right and the place is within the commit tolerance. "Lateral" scouts are released 3 units along the free scout's line, 1 (or 2) above and below it. "Blind" is the average over every pair of scouts on an 11 x 7 lattice, "best" the maximum. Source: `validate_survey::arrow_report`; the same helpers are used by `tests/survey.rs`.

## Legacy: trail-similarity model

The first validator. It compares *whole trails* as an ideal solver would, knows only a repeller, and was used to choose level 1's first six positions, which are the ones listed here (**not** the level's current positions: those were changed so that the free scout shows arrows at once, which this model, by construction, reads as "the free scout settles it"). It is kept so that the model itself stays tested, and is not evidence about the current levels.
